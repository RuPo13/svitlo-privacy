Privacy Policy

“Svitlo” app (ua.svitlo.umanska45)
Last updated: November 13, 2026

This policy explains what data the “Svitlo” app (the “App”) processes and how it is used. In short: we have no server of our own, we do not create user accounts, and we do not collect your personal data.

1. Who is responsible

Developer: Ruslan Ponomarov.
Contact for privacy questions: conductordesistemasweb@gmail.com.

2. Data stored on your device

The App stores the following locally in your phone's storage and does not send it to the developer:

  • the addresses you added (name, type, Telegram channel name);
  • reminder settings (how many minutes in advance to warn, on or off);
  • the most recently downloaded power outage schedule.

This data stays on your device. You can delete it by removing the addresses in Settings or by uninstalling the App.

3. Network requests

  • Telegram. To get the schedule, the App opens the public page of the channel you specified (t.me/s/<channel name>). Telegram, like any web server, can see your device's IP address and the channel name. We do not sign in to any Telegram account and have no access to your messages or contacts. Telegram's handling of this data is governed by Telegram's privacy policy.
  • Purchases (RevenueCat). The App uses RevenueCat to manage subscriptions. RevenueCat receives an anonymous user identifier, purchase and trial information, and technical device data (platform, app version). Payment details (card, etc.) are handled by Google Play or the App Store; we never see them. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

4. Notifications

The App asks for notification permission so it can warn you about power outages at the time you choose. Reminders are scheduled locally on your phone, without a server. You can change this permission in your system settings. On Android, the App also uses the exact alarm permission (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM) so reminders arrive on time.

5. Clipboard

The App reads the clipboard only when you tap the paste button for the channel name, and writes to it only when you tap the copy button. Clipboard contents are not stored or sent anywhere.

6. What we do not do

  • we do not collect your name, e-mail, phone number, location, contacts, or photos;
  • we do not show ads or use advertising identifiers;
  • we do not use our own analytics or trackers;
  • we do not sell or share data with third parties, other than the services in section 3 that are needed for the App to work.

7. Children

The App is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Retention and deletion

Local data is kept until you delete it in the App or uninstall the App. Purchase data is kept by RevenueCat and the app store under their own policies. To request deletion of purchase-related data, write to the address in section 1.

9. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data by contacting the address in section 1. Because we do not store personal data on a server, you control most of the data yourself on your device.

10. Changes to this policy

If the policy changes, we will update the date on this page. Significant changes will be mentioned in the App's release notes.
